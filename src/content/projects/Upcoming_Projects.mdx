## About the Project

I started with positioning a camera, light and some spheres.
![My first raytraced spheres](/Raytracing1.png)

Then I added quadrics and transformations, the Cook-Torrance lighting model and CSG. 
I used the CSG operation "difference" to put a cylinder in my sphere and create a hole with it. 
![My first raytraced spheres](/Raytracing2.png)

Shadows, reflection, refraction rays,transparent objects.
![My first raytraced spheres](/Raytracing3.png)

And at the end i added some animation, supersampling, depth of field and fog.
![My first raytraced spheres](/Raytracing4.gif)
